    A few useful things to know +

    Choose images, set a longest edge and format, then select Prepare images. Click an image in your list to compare its original and prepared copy. Use Actual size to check fine details, scrolling within each frame where needed. At 100%, one image pixel uses one CSS pixel; browser zoom and display scaling still apply.

    Downloads are new still images. Embedded metadata is not copied, and colour may vary. JPEG fills transparent areas with white. PNG keeps transparency and does not use a quality setting. A prepared copy can sometimes be larger than its original.

    Stopping lets the current decoder finish, discards its unfinished result, then waits for you to resume. Your files stay in this browser. There are no uploads, accounts or saved image history. Download the copies you want to keep before clearing the batch or leaving the page. Prepared files are limited to 100 MiB in total; ZIP headers count towards its 100 MiB limit. If a full batch will not fit, remove a copy or download images individually.
